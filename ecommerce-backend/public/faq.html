Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping with us

How do I track my order?
You can track all your past and ongoing orders by navigating to the My Orders page. You will see live delivery status updates and your unique 6-digit Delivery OTP.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major payment methods including Instant UPI QR (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
What is the Delivery OTP?
The Delivery OTP is a 6-digit security pin generated when your order is placed. Share this code with the delivery executive only when the package arrives safely at your doorstep.
What if I need to change my delivery address?
You can update your delivery address directly from the My Orders page before your package is marked as Shipped.
How do I return or exchange an item?
We offer an easy 7-day return policy. Items must be unwashed with tags attached. You can cancel or initiate return directly from the My Orders dashboard.
