Privacy, Returns & Shipping Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Privacy Policy

Pathaan Collection respects your privacy. We collect your name, email, phone number, and delivery address exclusively for order processing, dispatch, delivery communication, and security verification. Your data is never sold to third parties and is encrypted via industry standard SSL.

2. Shipping & Delivery Policy

  • Processing Time: Orders are processed and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours of confirmation.
  • Estimated Delivery: Standard delivery takes 3 to 6 business days across India.
  • Shipping Charges: Free standard delivery is offered on all prepaid and eligible orders.
  • Delivery OTP: For your safety, every order generates a secure 6-digit OTP required upon delivery receipt.

3. 7-Day Hassle-Free Return & Refund Policy

  • Eligibility: Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery provided they are unused, unwashed, with original tags intact.
  • Refund Processing: Once received and inspected, refunds are initiated within 48 hours directly to the original payment source (or bank transfer for COD orders).
  • Damaged / Wrong Item: If you receive a damaged or incorrect package, contact our support team within 48 hours with a photo for instant exchange or 100% refund.

4. Contact Support

Have questions about your order or return? Reach out to our dedicated support team via email or our store contact section.
